# Reliability and operations requirements

Read before altering recovery, cleanup, observability, health, deployment,
documentation, or tests:

- `docs/requirement/04-non-functional-requirements.md`: reliability, stale jobs, retention, shutdown, security, metrics, and health checks.
- `docs/requirement/05-test-and-developer-handover.md`: acceptance scenarios, benchmark evidence, runbooks, ADRs, and definition of done.
- `docs/requirement/requirements-traceability-matrix.csv`: requirement-to-test traceability.

Do not trade away bounded resources, auditability, or sanitized observability.
